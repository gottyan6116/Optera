# OPTERA website design

The supplied brief is the specification. New Next.js App Router site for Japanese AI beginners. Preserve the supplied transparent OPTERA logo. Navy #172033, blue #2F6BFF, teal #1FC8B5, lime #C7F36B, background #F5F8FC. Japanese Noto Sans JP with geometric Latin typography; generous left-aligned editorial composition.

The revised hero follows the user's later direction referencing JAPAN AI and SHIFT AI: a white first view, bold Japanese headline, a substantial laptop/phone product demonstration and a clear learning proposition. Decorative floating notification cards and English micro-labels have been removed from the first view. Four interactive scenes retain CSS perspective, restrained ambient motion, pointer parallax and directional depth transitions. CSS 3D is explicitly permitted by the brief and eliminates WebGL downloads and context failures. Essential content renders server-side. Reduced motion disables automatic movement. Visibility, focus, hover, active gestures and explicit pause control suspend autoplay.

The curriculum now uses three full-width illustrated learning stages. Purpose/conditions lead to an AI answer; a wireframe becomes a responsive website; an application connects to database, authentication and AI services. Original inline SVG diagrams are rendered as full desktop illustrations or simplified mobile diagrams with readable labels. They are instructional diagrams, not fabricated course screenshots. External reference sites' claims, results, awards and media are not reused.

All 12 sections follow the brief. Projects are labeled samples. Unconfirmed course operations and fees are identified as under preparation. Contact uses a shared validator, same-origin secure server route and optional configured authenticated HTTPS webhook. Without a destination the API returns 503 and never claims receipt. No service credentials were provided.

Existing logo is used unchanged. No invented organization facts, pricing, outcomes or working demos. Domain metadata and analytics are conditional on environment configuration.
